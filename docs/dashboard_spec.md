# Dashboard Specification
## Big Billion Days Regional Performance Review

### 1. Dashboard Objective
The dashboard analyzes completed orders, regional revenue,
sales representative performance, and monthly order trends.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
| KPI                     | Value  |
|-------------------------|-------|
| Total Completed Orders  | 63    |
| Total Completed Revenue | 346200 |

Revenue is calculated using the amount field for completed orders.

### 3. Dashboard Charts

#### Chart 1: Regional Revenue by Region
- Chart type: Clustered Column Chart
- Category: Region
- Value: Sum of Amount
- Purpose: Compare completed revenue across East, North,
  South, and West regions.

#### Chart 2: Revenue by Sales Representative
- Chart type: Clustered Bar Chart
- Category: Sales Representative
- Value: Sum of Amount
- Purpose: Compare the revenue generated by each sales
  representative.

#### Chart 3: Completed Orders by Month
- Chart type: Clustered Column Chart
- Category: Order Month
- Value: Count of Order ID
- Purpose: Identify changes in completed order volume
  across months.

### 4. Business Insights
1. The dataset contains 63 completed orders with total
   recorded revenue of 346200.
2. Regional revenue comparisons help identify regions
   contributing more or less to total revenue.
3. Sales representative comparisons help evaluate
   individual revenue contributions.
4. Monthly order counts help identify changes in demand
   and periods that may require additional planning.

Detailed regional, representative, and monthly values
should be interpreted from the PivotTables and charts.

### 5. Recommendations

1. Review regional revenue regularly and investigate
   regions with lower revenue to identify improvement
   opportunities.
2. Study the performance of sales representatives and
   share effective sales practices across the team.
3. Use monthly order trends to improve inventory planning,
   staffing, and promotional timing.

### 6. Data Source
- File: data/orders_clean.csv
- Completed orders: 63
- Revenue field: amount
- The cleaned dataset is used for reporting and
  reconciliation.

### 7. Validation
The completed order count should equal 63.
The sum of the amount field for completed orders should
equal 346200.
The dashboard figures should reconcile with the SQL
query results.
